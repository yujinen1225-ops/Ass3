Shipping Policy

Nimbu Active aims to deliver your order in 3 to 5 business days depending on seasonal demand. We use trusted delivery partners including Australia Post, TNT, DHL and other selected couriers depending on location and the size of your order.

Refund Policy

We have a 7 day return policy for damaged or faulty items only, In the unlikely event an item arrives damaged or faulty, please submit a return request within 48hrs of receiving your order. You have 7 days after your return request has been approved to send the order back.

To be eligible for a return, your item must be in the same condition that you received it, unworn or unused, with tags, and in its original packaging. You’ll also need the receipt (email) or proof of purchase.

To start a return, you can contact us by message If your return is accepted, we’ll send you a return instructions on how and where to send your package. Items sent back to us without first requesting a return will not be accepted.
